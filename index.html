How we make apps

.offline
Everything works without a connection. Your data is a file on your phone, and backups are plain zip files you keep wherever you like.
.open
The code is open. Read it, build it yourself, or send a fix or an idea to the app's repository.
.private
No accounts, no ads, no tracking, and this website sets no cookies either.
